# Copilot for Business — a two-hour overview

The business version of this course. **One sitting, about two hours, no coding.** It's for someone who will use Copilot for everyday work — email, documents, meetings, numbers, research — and wants to use it *well*: get good output quickly, catch it when it's wrong, and keep company information safe.

It borrows the three habits the full course spends twelve weeks on, in miniature:

1. **Write your own answer first, then check Copilot against it.** You can't judge output you couldn't have judged without it.
2. **Facts come from you; Copilot supplies structure, wording, and coverage.** Every name, number, date and quote in something you send is one you checked.
3. **Copilot is a fast first draft, never the final word.** You own whatever goes out under your name.
